Καλώς ήρθατε! Welcome!

The St. George Greek School offers a nurturing and engaging learning environment where students can discover their heritage as they study the Greek language and culture of our ancestors.

We offer Greek education from Kindergarten to 6th grade learning level. Students are placed according to their level of Greek language ability, so every child learns at the right pace.

What Our Students Learn

Taught at all levels throughout the school year.

Alphabet & Reading

From first letters to confident reading of Greek words and stories.

Writing & Grammar

Building strong writing skills and an understanding of Greek grammar.

Conversation & Vocabulary

Speaking practice through everyday conversations, plus the words and expressions to talk in Greek with family and friends.

Faith & Traditions

Religious and cultural traditions of the Greek Orthodox Church.

History

Lessons on Greek history and the stories of our ancestors.

Programs

Students perform at the OXI Day, Christmas and March 25th programs.

Our Learning Resources

In class and at home, our students learn with the books and online platforms of two trusted Greek-language programs.

Greek123

A proven step-by-step method to Greek fluency, combining books with a digital learning platform.

Visit greek123.com ↗

Ellinopoula

An e-learning platform that makes Greek exciting for kids with interactive lessons, games, videos and culture.

Visit ellinopoula.com ↗
