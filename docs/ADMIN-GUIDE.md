# Updating the Greek School website

A short guide for the school director and staff. You don't need any technical knowledge.

## Signing in

1. Go to **greekschool.stgeorgenwi.org/admin**. It takes you straight to the editor at app.pagescms.org.
2. Sign in.
   - **If you were invited by email:** type your email address. Pages CMS emails you a 6-digit code, valid for 5 minutes. Type the code to sign in.
   - **If you have a GitHub account:** you can sign in with GitHub instead.
3. Open **GreeKschool**. The menu on the left lists the parts of the website.

After you click **Save**, the website updates in **1–2 minutes**. Refresh the page to see the change. If you still see the old version, press Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac).

> **Everything you save is public at once.** There is no draft or approval step. Read your change through before you click **Save**.

## The calendar

The calendar has five parts, listed under **School Calendar**.

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Regular class schedule** | Class days and times for the year. Classes appear on the calendar automatically. |
| **Closures & class changes** | Days with no class, and extra or make-up classes. These replace the regular schedule. |
| **Events & programs** | Programs, feast days, celebrations, meetings, fundraisers and trips. |
| **Deadlines** | Registration, tuition, forms and sign-ups. |
| **Academic calendar PDF & settings** | The official PDF, plus a few display settings. |

The website sorts everything by date. Visitors can choose between the **Interactive calendar** (month, week or year view) and the **Academic calendar**, which is a list of dates with the PDF.

### Add an event (for example, the Christmas program)
1. Open **School Calendar → Events & programs**, then click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: for example "Christmas Program".
   - **Date**.
   - **Category**: Special program / celebration.
   - Optionally, a time, location, description, PDF or web link.
3. Click **Save**.

### Cancel a class (snow day, voting day, etc.)
1. Open **School Calendar → Closures & class changes**, then click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: for example "Snow day".
   - **What happens**: No class, or School closed (weather or emergency).
   - **Date**.
3. Click **Save**. The class disappears from that day, and the day shows "No class".

Also post an **Announcement** so families notice the change.

### Change a class date
Do both steps in **Closures & class changes**:
1. Add the old date with **What happens** set to **No class**.
2. Add the new date with **What happens** set to **Extra or make-up class**. You can enter a different time if needed.

### Add a school holiday or break
In **Closures & class changes**, choose **School holiday** or **Break**.

For a break of several days, fill in **Last day** as well. Every class day in between is cancelled. Use **Custom date text** to show something like "Dec 22 & 29" in the list.

### Set a registration deadline
1. Open **School Calendar → Deadlines** and add the deadline. Set **Kind** to **Registration**.
2. Open **Registration & Tuition** and set **Enrollment deadline** and the **Deadline sentence**. This updates the registration badge and the dashed box.

### Start a new school year
1. **Regular class schedule:** change the first day, last day and class times.
2. **Closures, Events and Deadlines:** delete last year's entries and add the new ones.
3. Upload the new PDF (see the next section).
4. **School Information:** update the school year text.
5. **Registration & Tuition:** update the dates and the form.

### Replace the academic calendar PDF
1. Open **School Calendar → Academic calendar PDF & settings**.
2. Upload the new PDF and click **Save**.

Both calendar buttons, **View Academic Calendar (PDF)** and **Download PDF**, now open the new file. Also update the matching entry in **Documents & Forms**.

### "Coming up" boxes
The boxes above the calendar show the next class and the next few important dates. To keep a minor item out of them, switch on **Don't show in "Coming up"** for that entry.

## Seasonal decorations

Open **Seasonal Look** in the menu. It has three parts:

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Right now** | Choose between automatic, one occasion of your choice, or the normal website. It also has the appearance settings that apply to every occasion. |
| **Occasions calendar** | The list of occasions and their dates. Each entry also says whether it shows a greeting. |
| **My custom celebration** | Your own design: colour, drawings and greeting. |

Normally you leave **Right now** set to **Automatic**. The website then changes by itself through the year, following the Occasions calendar. That calendar is filled in through January 2028 with all US federal holidays, the main Greek national and church days, school milestones and the four seasons.

### Preview first
Nothing changes for visitors while you preview. Add `?season=` and an occasion name to the website address, for example:

`https://greekschool.stgeorgenwi.org/?season=christmas`

A small **Preview** panel appears. Use it to try every occasion, animation and intensity.

### Hide or change the greeting of one occasion (for example, Autumn)
1. Open **Seasonal Look → Occasions calendar** and click the entry, for example "Autumn 2026".
2. Set **Greeting bar** to the option you want:
   - **Show the standard greeting**: the ready-made greeting in English and Greek.
   - **Show my own greeting**: type your text in **My own greeting**.
   - **No greeting**: the decorations stay, with no greeting bar.
3. Click **Save**.

### Hide all greetings
Open **Seasonal Look → Right now** and switch off **Show greeting bars**.

Visitors can also close a greeting with **✕**. It then stays closed on their device until the greeting changes.

### Show one occasion now (for example, Christmas)
1. Open **Seasonal Look → Right now**.
2. Set **What should the website show?** to **One occasion that I choose below**.
3. Under **The occasion I choose**, pick **Christmas** and choose a greeting.
4. Optionally add **Show from / Show until** dates. Outside those dates the website goes back to Automatic.

### Turn on snowfall, or change the effects
Open **Seasonal Look → Right now → Appearance**:
- **What to show:** everything, colours and decorations only, and so on.
- **Animation:** "Default" uses snow for Christmas and Winter, leaves for Autumn, and so on. Choose "Snowfall" to add snow to any occasion, or "No animation".
- **How much animation:** "A little" is recommended.
- **Decorations:** "Full" or "Light".

You can also set **Animation** for a single entry in the Occasions calendar.

Animations never run for visitors whose device asks for reduced motion, and every visitor has a **Pause animation** button.

### Return to the normal website
In **Right now**, set **What should the website show?** to **Nothing: the normal website**, then click **Save**.

### Add or change an occasion in the calendar
1. Open **Occasions calendar** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Give it a name for the list, for example "Pascha 2028". Choose the occasion, first day and last day.
3. Click **Save**.

The order of the list doesn't matter. When two entries overlap, the shorter one wins, so a one-day holiday always beats the season around it.

**Once a year**, add the next year's dates. Pascha, Clean Monday, Palm Sunday and the Monday holidays change every year. If April 23 falls before Pascha, St. George is celebrated on Bright Monday.

### Your own celebration
Fill in **My custom celebration**: colour, drawings, detail under the menu, animation, icon and greeting. Then choose **My custom celebration** as the occasion, either in **Right now** or in the Occasions calendar.

## Faculty
Open **Faculty** to replace the group photo or change the names.
- **Order:** drag the names so they match the photo from left to right.
- **Adding or removing people:** use the buttons under the list.
- **Hiding the section:** switch off **Show the faculty section**.
- **After a new photo:** if the names no longer sit under each person, adjust **Space at the sides of the names row**.

## Photo captions
Each photo has an English caption and an optional Greek caption. Both show under the photo in the viewer.

## Photos — please read

- **A photo is public the moment you upload it.** This is true even if it is not shown in the gallery. A deleted photo also stays in the website's history.
- **Upload only photos that are already approved.** The families must have consented, the children's faces must be blurred, and there must be no names. The **Show in the gallery** switch only takes a photo off the page. It does not make the file private.
- **Remove location data before you upload.** Turn off location for the camera on your phone. The website removes location data after upload, but the original file stays in the history.
- **If a family asks for a photo to be removed,** delete its entry in **Photo Gallery**. Then ask the website administrator to delete the file and, if needed, purge it from the history.

### Add a photo to the gallery
1. Open **Photo Gallery** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Upload the photo. Add a caption and category if you like.
3. Switch on **Show in the gallery** and click **Save**.

**To change the order,** drag the entries.

## Other everyday tasks

- **Post an announcement:** open **Announcements**, add an entry, and fill in **Publish date** and **Remove after**. Switch on **Mark as important** for urgent news.
- **Update tuition:** open **Registration & Tuition → Tuition boxes**.
- **Replace the registration form:** upload it under **Registration & Tuition → Enrollment form**, and also in **Documents & Forms**.
- **Change hours, phone, email or director:** open **School Information**.

**File types:** images must be JPG, PNG or WebP. Documents must be PDF, Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx). Other types are refused.

## If something goes wrong

Every save is kept as a version, so nothing is lost. Your website administrator can undo any change. If a change does not appear after 5 minutes, contact them.
