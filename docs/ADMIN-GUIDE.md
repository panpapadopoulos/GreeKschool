# Updating the Greek School website

A short guide for the school director and staff. You don't need any technical knowledge.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Sign in. Use **Sign in with GitHub** if you have a GitHub account. If you were invited by email, enter your email address and click the link that Pages CMS sends you.
3. Open **GreeKschool**. The menu on the left lists the sections of the website.

After you click **Save**, the change appears on the website in **1–2 minutes**. Refresh the page to see it. If you still see the old version, press Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac).

## Common tasks

### Post an announcement (for example, tomorrow's school closure)
1. Open **📣 Announcements** and click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: for example "No classes tomorrow".
   - **Type**: School Closure.
   - **Mark as important**: switch it on for urgent news.
   - **Publish date**: today.
   - **Remove after**: the last day the notice matters. It disappears automatically after that day.
   - **Message**: a few sentences.
3. Click **Save**.

You can write an announcement in English or Greek. Choose the language under **Language**. You can also add a picture or a PDF letter.

To remove an announcement early, open it and delete the entry, or set **Remove after** to yesterday.

### Upload a new academic calendar
1. Open **🏫 School Information**.
2. Under **Academic calendar (PDF)**, upload the new PDF.
3. Click **Save**. The **View Academic Calendar (PDF)** button now opens the new file.
4. Open **📄 Documents & Forms**, choose the calendar entry, replace its file and update its title. Click **Save**.

### Replace the registration form
Open **📝 Registration & Tuition**, upload the new PDF under **Enrollment form**, then click **Save**. Also update the matching entry in **📄 Documents & Forms**.

### Update the tuition amount
In **📝 Registration & Tuition**, open **Tuition boxes**, change the **Price**, then click **Save**.

### Add an event to the calendar (for example, the Christmas celebration)
1. Open **📅 School Calendar**. Under **Events**, click **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Christmas Program.
   - **Date**: the day of the event.
   - **Category**: Celebration / Program.
   - Optionally, add a time, a location or a short description.
3. Click **Save**.

You can add events in any order. The website sorts them by date and groups them by month.

### Start a new school year
- **🏫 School Information**: update the school year and the first and last day.
- **📝 Registration & Tuition**: update the deadline, the deadline sentence and the form.
- **📅 School Calendar**: delete the old events and add the new ones.
- Upload the new calendar PDF, as described above.

### Turn on snowfall for December
1. Open **❄️ Seasonal Theme**.
2. Do one of the following:
   - **Easiest:** set **Mode** to *Automatic*. The schedule already shows Christmas from December 1 to January 6.
   - **Right now:** set **Mode** to *Use the theme I choose below* and **Theme to use** to *Christmas*.
3. Click **Save**.

### Turn off snowfall after Christmas
- With the automatic schedule, snowfall stops by itself after the end date.
- To stop it right away, set **Mode** to *Off*, or switch off **Animated effects**. Click **Save**.

Use **Effect intensity** to make the snow lighter or heavier. *Light* is recommended.

**Preview a theme before turning it on.** Add `?season=christmas` to the website address, for example `https://greekschool.stgeorgenwi.org/?season=christmas`. The other theme names are `autumn`, `independence`, `oxi`, `easter` and `backtoschool`. Only you see the preview, and it changes nothing for anyone else.

### Add photos to the gallery
> **Privacy comes first.** Upload only photos of students whose parents have given consent, with children's faces blurred. Never add names. Everything you upload becomes public, so never upload unapproved or unblurred originals.

1. Open **🖼️ Photo Gallery**. Under **Photos**, click **Add an entry**.
2. Upload the photo. Large photos are made smaller automatically.
3. Optionally, add a caption and a category, for example "Christmas Program".
4. Switch on **Approved for publication**. Photos without it stay hidden.
5. Click **Save**.

**To change the order**, drag the photos in the list. **To remove a photo**, delete its entry. If a family asks for a photo to be taken down, also ask your website contact to delete the file itself.

### Change school hours, email, phone or director
Open **🏫 School Information**, change the field and click **Save**.

## If something goes wrong
Every change is saved as a version in GitHub, so nothing is ever lost. Your website contact can restore an earlier version at any time. If a change does not appear after 5 minutes, contact them.
